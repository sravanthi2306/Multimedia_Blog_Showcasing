Annamayya            is a 1997 Indian Telugu-language biographical film directed by K. Raghavendra Rao and produced by V. Doraswamy Raju. The film stars Nagarjuna Akkineni as the 15th-century Saint Annamacharya, alongside Suman, Mohan Babu, Ramya Krishna, and Bhanupriya in supporting roles. The movie chronicles the life, spiritual journey, and devotion of Annamacharya toward Lord Venkateswara of Tirupati. Composed by M. M. Keeravani, the iconic soundtrack features legendary devotional songs and keertanas that became massive cultural hits. The film received widespread critical acclaim, winning two National Film Awards and eight Nandi Awards. It remains one of the most celebrated biographical devotional classics in South Indian cinema history.

2. KGF Chapter 2

K.G.F: Chapter 1            is a 2018 Indian Kannada-language period action film written and directed by Prashanth Neel and produced by Vijay Kiragandur. The film stars Yash as Raja Krishnappa Bairya (Rocky), alongside Srinidhi Shetty, Ramachandra Raju, and Anant Nag in prominent roles. The story revolves around Rocky, a poverty-stricken young man who rises through the ranks of the Mumbai underworld on a quest for immense power to fulfill a promise made to his dying mother. He infiltrates the deadly Kolar Gold Fields to assassinate the oppressive leader Garuda and liberate the enslaved workers. Composed by Ravi Basrur, the iconic background score and powerful dialogues became a massive phenomenon across India. The film won two National Film Awards and emerged as a historic blockbuster in Indian cinema.
